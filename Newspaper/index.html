Community Garden Brings Neighbors Together
Local residents unite to grow food and friendship
By Edna Smith

The community garden project started as a small idea but has quickly become a centerpiece of the neighborhood. Residents from all walks of life gather each weekend to plant, water, and harvest fresh vegetables together.

What began as an effort to improve local access to healthy food has blossomed into a source of friendship, cooperation, and pride. The garden stands as a testament to the community’s resilience and Edna’s resourcefulness.
